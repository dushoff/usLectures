Types of data

	Nominal: names only, no order

		NOANS

	Ordinal: order, no differences

		NOANS

	Interval: differences, no ratios

		NOANS

	Ratio

		NOANS

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Numeric variables

	Interval and ratio variables are usually numeric

	These can be stored as continuous numbers or integers; it often
	won't matter to us

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Non-numeric variables

	Have two representations:

		The name is a character

		The list of legal names is a factor

	R needs to convert characters to factors to analyze or plot
