SEC Working with R and rstudio

----------------------------------------------------------------------

R is a programming language

	Whether you are writing programs or not, you should understand
	roughly what that means

	A program is a collection of simple instructions (read, write, send,
	manipulate, calculate) to be executed in a logical order

	A program's flow is controlled by \emph{control structures}
	(instructions about what instruction to do next)

Principles and practice

	Your project should be

		Replicable

			Restart R and run everything again to test

			Don't do `rm' instead

		Shareable

			Use R projects and the `here' package

			Don't use absolute file paths like `C:myName/myDirectory'

		Traceable

			Use version control!

----------------------------------------------------------------------

R is old-fashioned

	Does not distinguish between values and vectors of values!

		Tries to figure out what you want

	Does not require print() most of the time

	Tries to figure out when a command is done instead of making you
	signal

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Scripting should be structured

	R and its ancestors were developed when people were using tiny
	terminals

		... or punch cards!

	Try to avoid these tricks and shortcuts; they can lead to trouble

SEC Tidyverse

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Functions

	R comes with a \emph{lot} of useful functions

	Others can be loaded from “packages”

	You add new packages to your “library” with `install.packages()'

	And load them into your R session with `library()'

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Tidyverse

	tidyverse is a \emph{group} of packages designed to allow data
	manipulation and visualization at a higher conceptual level than
	base R

		works primarily on tables

		focuses on logical structure
