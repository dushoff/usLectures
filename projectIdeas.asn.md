
Our goal will be to choose some project ideas that appeal to you as a group, and to give most of you an opportunity to try to find a group that interests you scientifically or statistically.

Provide an answer to _each_ of the _numbered_ items. Answers can be brief, or you can add detail if you like. _These answers will not commit you to anything_.

1. Describe a scientific system that could potentially interest you for a project (for example, social behaviour in fruit flies, or seed production in maple trees).

1. Describe a scientific question that could potentially interest you. This can be for the system above, or for a different system (for example, are male bees more likely to prefer pollen over nectar than female bees are? Does phosphorous addition increase algal growth in Ontario lakes?)

1. Identify an available data set that you might want to study. You should be able to find interesting data sets online. If you have, or expect to have your own data set, or can get one from a lab where you are working, you can discuss that instead.
