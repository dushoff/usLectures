
For your group project you will:
1. Form a group
1. Submit a suggested topic, with some ideas about a scientific question and statistical analysis (18 Nov)
1. Submit an analysis plan (25 Nov)
1. Submit a final report (4 Dec)
1. Give a 10-minute presentation, with each group member presenting for 1.5–2 min (week of 7 Dec)

You can use a real or simulated dataset. If you are planning an experiment, we can help you simulate possible outcomes.

The final report should be 5-10 pages, including pictures. It's OK to include supplemental material, but the report should speak for itself, and supplement should be clearly marked. Describe your data, a scientific question, and a statistical approach. one or two main statistical tests and possibly some exploratory tests.
