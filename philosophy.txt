UNIT 3: Statistical philosophy

----------------------------------------------------------------------

TSEC Overview

	We use statistics to confirm effects, estimate parameters, and
	predict outcomes

	I think smoking is bad for lung capacity in children

		\emph{Confirmation:} Children who smoke have lower exhalation
		volumes (ev)

		\emph{Estimation:} The average non-smoker has an ev 1.1--1.3
		times higher than a smoker

		\emph{Prediction:} A 14-year-old male smoker will smoker will
		have an ev of 2.6--3.0 L/s
