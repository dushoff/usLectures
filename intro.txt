	You will do best if you take notes

		You should know by now what works for you

		Or else that you need to keep working on it

What you need to know

	I am still designing this course, based on what you-all know and
	what you want to learn

	My basic goals are

		Build computer and data-management skills

		Convince you that you need a statistical philosophy to do science

			Learn basic principles of this philosophy

		Discuss principles of different tests

			linear models, generalized linear models, permutation tests

			random effects and mixed models

			maybe other things
