What is the definition of a P value?

	STUDENTS It is a probability

	What probability?

		STUDENTS The probability that there is not actually an effect?

			CONV No. We will talk more about this

		STUDENTS The probability of seeing a value as (or more) extreme as what
		you got --  \emph{if} the null hypothesis is true

----------------------------------------------------------------------

I measure plant growth in plots where I did and did not add K

	What are some concerns before I even analyze the data?

		STUDENTS What other factors could affect the outcome

			STUDENTS E.g., what are pre-existing differences if any

	What does it mean if P $< 0.05$?

		STUDENTS Statistically significant

	What does it mean if P $> 0.05$?

		STUDENTS Not statistically significant
